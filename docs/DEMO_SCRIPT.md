# Ghost demo · 2 minutes 45 seconds

## Before presenting

Run `npm run dev` or open the packaged Ghost app. Use local detection for a fully offline presentation. Reset Demo in Settings if needed. No API key or permissions are required for the sample flow. Keep the window at least 1280 × 860.

## 0:00–0:15 · The insight

“Everyone is building AI that does what you ask. But there's a problem: you first have to realize what AI could help you with.

Ghost works differently.

It understands how you work, finds the repetitive workflows hiding in your day, and shows you what you should never have to do again.”

## 0:15–0:35 · Establish trust

Click **Try Demo** or **Explore Demo Mode**.

“This is a clearly labeled sample workday so you can see weeks of context in seconds. Outside Demo Mode, Ghost actually observes the foreground Mac app. It doesn’t record keystrokes or screenshots. Window titles are optional.”

Show **Activity**, then return **Home**.

## 0:35–1:00 · Discover

Click **Analyze patterns**.

“Ghost finds a recurring reporting workflow: analytics, email, then Notion. The same sequence appears four times. We didn’t tell it to automate a report; the pattern gave us the starting point.”

Open **Weekly reporting** using **Explore**.

## 1:00–1:25 · Explain

Expand **See the evidence**.

“Here are the actual sample sessions supporting the suggestion. This workflow averages 38 minutes. At one run a week, that is about 33 hours a year of potential savings. These are estimates—not a claim that we’ve already recovered that time.”

“The local detector always works. If you enable AI, it interprets this structured context; it never needs screenshots.”

## 1:25–1:55 · Approve

Click **Automate this**. Let the steps reveal.

“Ghost turns discovery into a proposed workflow. I can edit the steps, and nothing becomes active without approval.”

Click **Activate workflow**, then **View your automation**.

“This is now a real saved workflow. The prototype runs drafts manually. Scheduled collection from connected apps is the next step.”

## 1:55–2:20 · Produce something real

Click **Inspect & run draft**, then **Generate local draft**.

“These are editable sample metrics and updates. The local runner organizes them into a report draft and review checklist. It doesn’t pretend to fetch data from services we haven’t connected.”

Show the generated draft. Close the modal and return **Home**.

## 2:20–2:45 · Close

“The dashboard now reflects the workflow’s estimated weekly saving. Everything persists, and I can pause observation, inspect the evidence, or delete my history.

The opportunity isn’t another place to type prompts. It’s the work we haven’t thought to question.

Ghost. Works ahead of you.”

## Recovery

- No opportunity: load sample workday, then Analyze patterns.
- Already activated: sidebar **Reload sample workday** resets sample workflows.
- AI request failure: local results remain available; no recovery needed.
- Closed window: use the Ghost menu-bar icon → Open Ghost.
